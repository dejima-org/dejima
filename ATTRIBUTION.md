## Authorship

All logos, assets, and artwork are human-made. No AI tools were used in their
creation. The artist is anonymous.

## What is covered

Both marks, in every form:

- the logo: the filled tiles and the outline variants, in both light-mode and
  dark-mode renderings, at any resolution, and the favicon;
- the wordmark: the solid and outline renderings, again in both modes.

The terms attach to the marks rather than to this list, so renderings not
listed here are covered too.
